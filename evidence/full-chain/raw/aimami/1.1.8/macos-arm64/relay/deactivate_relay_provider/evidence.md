## Delta summary vs 1.0.9 baseline

The concrete code-level delta mirrors the pattern already documented for `load_relay_state`/`activate_relay_provider`/`diagnose_codex_router` this round: the `apiKey` field scrubbing has been centralized into the shared `storage::sanitize_for_export` function (same VA `0x1004b14b0` confirmed as a 5th call site here) rather than living as inline per-command logic.
